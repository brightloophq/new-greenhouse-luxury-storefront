# Luxury Design Principles

Non-negotiable design law for The New Greenhouse. These apply to **every** screen, section, and component. Treat them as acceptance criteria, not suggestions.

1. **Never place two buttons beside each other** unless absolutely necessary. Prefer one primary action; demote the second to a text link or vertical stack.
2. **Every section must breathe.** Generous vertical rhythm; no cramped stacking.
3. **Typography creates hierarchy.** Size, weight, and spacing establish order — not boxes, borders, or color alone.
4. **Images are the product.** Photography leads; let it fill and carry the layout.
5. **White space is content.** Emptiness is intentional and composed, never "unfinished."
6. **One focal point per viewport.** Each screenful has a single thing the eye goes to first.
7. **Never exceed 3 primary actions** on a screen.
8. **Premium brands remove elements more often than they add them.** When in doubt, take something away.

---

### How these are enforced
Every milestone in `REDESIGN-ROADMAP.md` must satisfy these before it is considered complete. Design review for any surface explicitly checks all 8. A section that fails any principle is not "done," regardless of functional correctness.
